# Example Co. Employee Handbook (sample)

> Fictional sample content so `make ingest` and `make ask` work out of the box.
> Replace this folder with your own documents, or point `DOCS_DIR` elsewhere.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours,
people choose when they work, as long as they are reachable for scheduled meetings.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year, plus public
holidays. Up to 5 unused vacation days roll over into the next year; anything above
that is forfeited on 31 December. Vacation requests of more than 5 consecutive days
should be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Expenses

Travel meals are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Hotels should be booked through the
company travel portal. Submit receipts in the expenses tool within 30 days of the
purchase; claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Equipment

Every new hire receives a laptop and a $500 home-office allowance, available during
their first six months. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
